About This Study

This research investigates whether localized analogy-based learning can improve students' understanding of Computer Studies concepts when compared with traditional English-based instruction.

You will study a lesson, take a quiz, and complete a questionnaire. Your responses will help determine which teaching approach is more effective.

Lessons You Will Learn

1. Introduction to Computers

Understanding what a computer is, its functions, and examples in everyday life.

2. Variables

Learning how data is stored and managed in programming.

3. Algorithms

Step-by-step procedures used to solve problems.

4. Flowcharts

Visual representation of processes and instructions.
