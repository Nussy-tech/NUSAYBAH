Learning Computer Studies Through Localized Analogies

CodeLokal is a research project that investigates how local language, cultural examples and familiar community experiences can improve students' understanding of Computer Studies concepts.

About CodeLokal

Many students struggle to understand abstract computing concepts because the explanations often use unfamiliar examples and technical terms.

This project explores whether localized analogies can improve learning, engagement and retention among secondary school students.

Research Question

Can localized analogy-based learning improve students' understanding of Computer Studies concepts compared to traditional English-based instruction?
